# Revenue Model

## 6 Revenue Streams

### 1. API Subscriptions (SaaS)
Monthly subscriptions for API access:
- **Free**: 100 calls/month (trial users)
- **Starter**: $49/month for 1,000 calls (indie developers)
- **Professional**: $199/month for 10,000 calls (trading firms)
- **Enterprise**: $999/month unlimited (hedge funds, priority support)

**How it works**: Developers integrate our API to get sentiment data. Charged monthly based on usage tier.

**Target**: 100 paid users by Year 1 = $10-30k MRR

---

### 2. Oracle-as-a-Service (B2B)
Prediction market platforms pay for automated oracle services:
- **Basic**: $500/month (1,000 queries included)
- **Pro**: $2,000/month (10,000 queries)
- **Enterprise**: $5,000/month (unlimited + SLA)

**How it works**: Platforms integrate our oracle to auto-resolve their prediction markets. We charge per query or flat monthly fee.

**Target**: 10 platform clients by Year 1 = $10-30k MRR

---

### 3. White-Label Solutions
Banks, exchanges, media companies get branded versions:
- **Setup Fee**: $5,000-50,000 (branding, deployment, training)
- **Monthly Maintenance**: $1,000-10,000 (hosting, support, updates)

**How it works**: Client gets their own branded sentiment platform. We handle backend, they use their logo/domain.

**Target**: 2 white-label clients by Year 1 = $4-20k MRR

---

### 4. Data Marketplace
Sell historical and real-time sentiment datasets:
- **Historical Data**: $100-5,000 per dataset (1-year sentiment for specific tickers)
- **Real-Time Feeds**: $200-2,000/month (WebSocket feeds for top stocks)
- **Backtesting Data**: $500-5,000 (5-year sentiment + price data)

**How it works**: Researchers, quant funds, analysts buy datasets for analysis and backtesting strategies.

**Target**: 50 sales/month = $10-20k MRR

---

### 5. Premium Features
Power users pay for advanced features:
- **Multi-Ticker Dashboard**: $29/month (compare 10 tickers side-by-side)
- **Sentiment Alerts**: $49/month (email/Telegram/SMS notifications)
- **Custom AI Prompts**: $99/month (tailor analysis to your style)
- **Priority Processing**: $149/month (sub-second responses)

**How it works**: In-app upsells. Users start free, upgrade for advanced features.

**Target**: 100 premium users = $5-15k MRR

---

### 6. Transaction Fees
Revenue share on prediction market betting volume:
- **Fee Structure**: 0.5-2% of total betting volume
- **Split**: 50/50 with platform (negotiable)
- **Automation**: Smart contracts enforce fee distribution

**Example**: Platform has $1M monthly betting volume → 1% fee = $10k → 50/50 split = $5k to us

**How it works**: Platforms that use our oracle share a small percentage of their betting fees. Automated via blockchain.

**Target**: $500k betting volume by Year 1 = $2.5-10k MRR

## Why This Model Works

✅ **Diversified**: 6 streams reduce dependency on any single source  
✅ **Scalable**: Low marginal cost per user (AI API costs ~$0.01 per analysis)  
✅ **Proven**: Similar to Chainlink (oracle services), Bloomberg (data subscriptions)  
✅ **High Margin**: 70-80% gross margins typical for SaaS  
✅ **Recurring**: Monthly subscriptions provide predictable revenue
